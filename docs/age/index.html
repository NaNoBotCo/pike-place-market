Age

The city's licence file carries a start date. Inside the district the median business has held the same licence for 24.7 years; 104 of 153 are past twenty, 34 are past forty, and 7 started in the last five.

Inside the line, and just outside it

The same measure for the 46 licensed businesses within a hundred and fifty metres of the boundary and outside it: a median of 18.7 years, 21 past twenty, 2 past forty.

Both columns count active licences only. A business that closed leaves the file, so neither column is a survival rate — it is the age of what is standing.
