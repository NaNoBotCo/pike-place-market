Are you inside it?

The district is a 30-cornered polygon with an area of 12.827 acres and 1,101 metres of edge. Standing anywhere near Pike Place, a step either way changes the answer.

The device's position is compared in the browser against the boundary file on this page. How the line is drawn.

Five counts of one place

  • 153hold a city business licence inside the linethe only dated, address-level record of who trades here
  • 473are on the Market's own vendor rosterincluding the daystall craftspeople and farmers, who hold no separate city licence

How long they stay

Median time inside the line, on the same licence: 24.7 years. In the hundred and fifty metres outside it: 18.7 years.

34 of the 153 businesses inside the boundary have held the same licence for more than forty years. Among the 46 licensed businesses within 150 m of the line and outside it, 2 have.

The whole distribution

One stall, in three records

Isellpictures.com

Fodoughgrafiks (Isellpictures.com) has been selling digital art in Pike Place Market since 2007. Owner Daniel T. Fleming shoots, edits, prints coats, trims, mounts, signs, and sells a body of work that celebrates the most iconic scenes and situations from the Greater PNW and beyond.

The Market's own description, quoted as published.

The city licensed FODOUGHGRAFIKS to FLEMING DANIEL T on 10 March 2006 — 20 years ago. The Market's own roster says the business has sold here since 2007. In May 2024 he put it at eighteen years. Three sources, two dates, one stall.
