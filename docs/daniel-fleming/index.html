Daniel T. Fleming

Fodoughgrafiks (Isellpictures.com) has been selling digital art in Pike Place Market since 2007. Owner Daniel T. Fleming shoots, edits, prints coats, trims, mounts, signs, and sells a body of work that celebrates the most iconic scenes and situations from the Greater PNW and beyond.

The Market's own description of the business, quoted as published.

What the records say

The City of Seattle licensed FODOUGHGRAFIKS to FLEMING DANIEL T on 10 March 2006, at 85 PIKE ST # 125. That licence has run 20.53 years, which makes his the 102nd-oldest of the 153 licences trading inside the district today.

The Market's own roster says the business has sold here since 2007. Asked in May 2024, he said eighteen years, which points back to 2006. Three records, two dates, one stall — and the licence is the one with a day on it.

Where he stands

Registered at 85 Pike St, the street number 20 licensed businesses share — the Market's own front door, and as precise as the city's record gets for a daystall. Category on the Market's roster:

The Crafts Market is the daystall system: a table rented by the day, assigned by seniority, under the Market's own rules rather than a shop lease. 383 of the 473 vendors on the Market's roster hold no separate city licence at a market address; his is one of the 90 that do.
